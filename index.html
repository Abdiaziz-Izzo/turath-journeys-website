FAQ

Common questions.

Why not just hire a driver?

You absolutely can. A driver can take you to Uhud, Quba, Qiblatayn and other landmarks. However, that's not what we do. We take you through the stories behind these places: the Seerah, the people, the events, the historical context and the lessons. With a driver, you'll simply be visiting these sites. With us, you experience them and understand the legacy that's been left behind.

How do I book a tour?

Message us directly on WhatsApp with your preferred date, group size, and which tour you're interested in. We'll confirm availability and finalise timings with you directly. No online booking or deposit required upfront.

Do you provide hotel pickup?

Yes. The Heritage & Ziyārah Tour includes pickup and drop-off from your hotel. The Masjid an-Nabawi walk begins on foot from the mosque area.

Are tours available for just women?

No. For our tours, females must be accompanied by a mahram (a male relative).

Can the tour be customised?

Yes. Every tour can be adapted around your interests, schedule and group: a family-friendly pace, a deeper dive for students of knowledge, or a plan built around limited time in the city.

Is this suitable for children or elderly guests?

Yes. Because tours are private and small-group, we can adjust the pace and walking distance to suit your group, including families and older guests.

What language are tours conducted in?

We specialise in and cater to Western visitors, primarily in the English language. For other languages, reach out on WhatsApp to inquire.

What does "Turath" mean?

The Prophet ﷺ said: "The scholars are the inheritors of the prophets." Turath means heritage: knowledge passed down from the Prophet ﷺ through the scholars. That's what we share. Not just information, but knowledge that transforms how you see Madinah, the Seerah, and your journey.
